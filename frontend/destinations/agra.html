🏛️ Agra – City of the Taj Mahal

🌟 Places to Explore

  • 🕌 Taj Mahal – symbol of eternal love
  • 🏰 Agra Fort – majestic red sandstone fortress
  • 🌼 Mehtab Bagh – view the Taj at sunset
  • ⛲ Itimad-ud-Daulah – “Baby Taj” marble beauty
  • 📸 Fatehpur Sikri – ancient royal complex

🎯 Top Activities

  • 🚕 Sunrise tour of Taj Mahal
  • 📷 Dress in Mughal attire for photos
  • 🛍️ Buy marble crafts and leather goods
  • 🌹 Romantic walks by Yamuna River

🗓️ Best Time to Visit

October to March – for cool weather and Taj sunrise magic

🏨 Recommended Hotels

  • 🌟 ITC Mughal – luxury with Taj view
  • 🛎️ Radisson Hotel – near Taj East Gate
  • 🏨 Crystal Sarovar – value for money

🍽️ What to Eat

  • 🍢 Mughlai Chicken & Kebab platters
  • 🍛 Royal thalis & biryani at Pinch of Spice
  • 🍬 Petha (famous sweet of Agra)

💰 Budget Estimate

₹6,000 – ₹10,000 for a 2-day trip per person
